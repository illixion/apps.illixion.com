Apps for Apple Vision Pro, iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch, made by one developer.

The code for every app here is public on GitHub. Most are not on the App Store yet, so the iPhone and iPad versions install through AltStore or SideStore.

Media

Browse, view and play your own photos, video and films.

Convolution

Photo, video and film library

Convolution is one library for pictures, video and films. Point it at your Photos library, a Stash, Nextcloud or Jellyfin server, or a folder of local files, and browse them in a grid that keeps each image's real proportions.

Films play with their object audio intact, so overhead sounds stay overhead and move with your head instead of being folded down to 7.1. On Vision Pro it also turns flat photos and videos into 3D. That part needs the headset; the iPhone and iPad app is the library, viewer and film player.

Runs on
Vision Pro, iPhone, iPad, Apple TV, Mac
Needs
Photos library, or a Stash, Nextcloud or Jellyfin server
  • Source on GitHub

Remote and streaming

Reach your computers and servers from wherever you are.

Longwave

Remote desktop and game streaming

Longwave connects you to your computers. It does VNC remote desktop, streams games from a Sunshine or GameStream host, and gives you an SSH terminal, with audio coming along.

On Vision Pro the desktops and terminals sit in the room as windows you place where you want them. On iPhone and iPad it is the same set of connections on a flat screen.

Runs on
Vision Pro, iPhone, iPad, Mac
Needs
A computer to connect to, on your network or over a VPN
  • Source on GitHub
  • Website

Listening

Audio you own, on every screen you have.

Worldcast

Single-user podcast listener

Worldcast is a podcast listener for one person. A small Node.js server you run keeps your subscriptions and progress, and a web app or the Apple clients play from it.

The sideloaded iPhone and iPad build leaves out the Apple Watch app, which would use up another of your free app slots.

Runs on
iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, Vision Pro
Needs
Your own Worldcast server (Node.js)
  • Source on GitHub

Sideloading

Install the iPhone and iPad apps without the App Store.

Add to AltStore Add to SideStore

Or paste this address into the Sources tab: https://apps.illixion.com/source.json

  1. Install AltStore or SideStore. Both read the same source.
  2. Add the source with the buttons above. They open the app on an iPhone.
  3. Tap Free on the app you want.

The apps are re-signed on your device with your own Apple ID. A free Apple ID allows three active sideloaded apps, and each has to be refreshed every seven days: AltStore does that through AltServer on a Mac or PC on your network, SideStore on the device. These builds need iOS 26 or later, and Worldcast needs 26.5.

This site is a static page and a JSON file, with no accounts and no analytics.
