How it works

  1. Install Apparition, AltStore or SideStore. All three read the same source.
  2. Add the source with the buttons above, which open AltStore or SideStore on an iPhone or iPad, or paste its address into Apparition.
  3. Tap Free on the app you want.

The apps are re-signed on your device with your own Apple ID. A free Apple ID allows three active sideloaded apps, each refreshed every seven days: AltStore does that through AltServer on a computer on your network, SideStore on the device, and Apparition either way: through its desktop app, from anywhere over Tailscale, or on the device with LocalDevVPN. This page is static, with no accounts and no analytics.
