How it works

  1. Install AltStore or SideStore. Both read the same source.
  2. Open the buttons above on your iPhone, or paste the source address into the app's Sources tab.
  3. Tap Free on the app you want.

Good to know

  • These builds are re-signed on your device with your own Apple ID. A free Apple ID allows three active sideloaded apps, and each one has to be refreshed every seven days. AltStore refreshes through AltServer on a Mac or PC on your network, SideStore on the device itself.
  • The apps need iOS 26 or later.
  • This site is a static page and a JSON file. It has no accounts and no analytics.
Updates appear in the app list on their own. The Mac and Apple TV builds are not in this source.
